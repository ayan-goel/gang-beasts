# Joining the next private-room round

A player who opens an invite during a match loads the room’s Skyline content and enters as a spectator. The server queues their connected seat for the next playable round. During the current round they can orbit the camera and watch the same score and physics as the fighters.

The simulation worker takes the waiting list on the last tick of a nonterminal round result. It includes competitors still entitled to their seats, including those inside disconnect grace, and adds connected spectators in ascending lobby-seat order. An entrant starts at zero. A former competitor who forfeited and rejoined has a new membership identity, even when their browser cookie, seat or color is the same. They enter with a new fighter. A spectator who leaves or loses the connection leaves the waiting list. A newer socket can register again. A stale close cannot withdraw the newer socket.

Each published frame retains the lineup used to build it. The socket sends the new assignment before that lineup’s first snapshot. Each browser installs a fresh timeline and input sampler, and acknowledges the new epoch. Input queues stay bound to stable lobby seats while compact fighter indices may move. A retired canvas cannot transmit a final control to its replacement. The current browser opts into this handoff with `lineups=1`. A player using the original handshake receives the existing return-to-lobby message, then a fresh assignment on their next socket. A match-winning result proceeds to rematch as usual. If fewer than two competitors remain available for another round, the room returns to the lobby.

## Verification

- Three independent Chromium profiles exercised a live invitation. Gold watched round one, pressed a movement key, and sent no gameplay input. After Blue ran off the roof, the first point stayed with Mint. All three browsers showed Gold at zero in round two and observed Gold walk more than one metre with their own key. The three room sockets stayed connected. The pre-change run reached round two with no Gold fighter. [Private-room late join](../../web/tests/online-late-join.spec.ts)
- A mixed-handshake run exercised the same promotion. The player using the original handshake received kind 27 and opened one replacement socket. The other two received kind 34 on their original sockets. All three still observed the newcomer’s movement.
- A former host reconnected within grace, then let a second disconnect expire. They rejoined as a spectator under a new name. Upon another departure they entered round two at zero and saw the remaining competitor keep their point. Leaving again awarded the survivor the next point and returned that player to the lobby with host privileges. [Seat expiry and reuse](../../web/tests/support/disconnectScenario.ts)
- Native tests cover withdrawing a waiter, replacing a socket, preserving an older published lineup and filling seven open spawns in reverse arrival order. The browser decoder rejects a superseded epoch, a different match serial and a first snapshot belonging to another roster epoch. [Lineup fixtures](../../crates/server/src/lineup.rs) · [Assignment decoder](../../web/src/online/MatchFeed.test.ts)
