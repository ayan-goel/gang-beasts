How to play

Invite your friends, ready up, and be the last bean standing. Each round takes you to a shuffled arena. The host picks the finish line: 3, 5, or 7 wins.

Move
WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to run.
Punch & grab
Tap J or K to punch. Hold to grab with your left or right hand.
Throw
Grab a rival, hold E to lift, then release your grip.
Headbutt
Press Q at close range.
Jump & climb
Press Space to jump. Grab a ledge and hold Space to pull yourself up.
Swim
Steer toward an edge and hold Space to surface before you drown.
Camera
Press Z or X to turn the view.
Leave a match
Press Esc to open the match menu.
